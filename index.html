Privacy Policy - Mochi Merge: Rainbow Jar

Last updated: October 28, 2026. Developer: Softbloom Games. Contact: a97g103@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores

Mochi Merge works without an account. Your progress (scores, coins, settings, friends' challenge codes) is stored on your device.

2. Information we may collect

  • World rankings (optional, off by default). If you turn on "Share my scores", we store an anonymous identifier, your nickname and your best scores on our servers (Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore) so that rankings and "Top X%" can be shown. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Account. To delete your ranking data, contact us.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, display name and email address, and we store a copy of your game progress in the cloud (Firebase) so you can continue on another device. You can sign out at any time. To delete the cloud data, contact us.
  • Advertising. The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and usage information to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Where required (EU/UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn which product was bought.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Sharing challenge codes

A challenge code contains your chosen nickname, score and a game seed. Do not use your real name as a nickname.

5. Your choices and rights

You can delete local game data by uninstalling the app. You can switch off world rankings, sign out of Google and manage ad personalization at any time. To access or delete data we hold about you, contact a97g103@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
